## Affected Pages/Routes
- `/` : the verdict word, stars and reason, the hour cells, the now strip, the 48 hour chart with its readout, three more days. Check in light and dark, at 320, 390 and 1280 px.
- `/week` : one section per day on a phone, one table on desktop. Check the fall-back day 2026-11-01 (25 hours) and the spring-forward day 2026-03-08 (23 hours).
- `/about` : sources and update times match what the server actually fetches; the wave-height note is present.
- `/assets/*` : hashed filenames, long cache, same origin.
- Any other path : 404.

## Key Interactions to Verify
- Touch scrub on the chart moves the readout; a vertical swipe starting on the chart scrolls the page and does not move the readout (`touch-action: pan-y`).
- Keyboard: the hidden "Forecast hour" range input is reachable by Tab, arrow keys change the hour, and the readout text is announced.
- The "Now" button returns to the first frame.
- The hour cells on `/` match the band under the chart hour for hour.

## Edge Cases
- Upstream down at load: the verdict and hour cells are hidden, raw numbers stay with an absolute "as of" time, and the amber rule and marker show with ink text.
- Cold cache right after a deploy: "Checking the water..." appears, then fills; no blank page.
- Null hours in the marine data: a dashed bridge and a shaded cell on the chart, "No forecast for this hour" in the readout, no numbers shown for it.
- Gap at the very start or end of the series: no bridge, shade only.
- Host in UTC at 11pm New York time: "Today" is still today, "Now" is the right hour.
- JavaScript off: the first frame of the readout is readable.
- No horizontal scroll at 320 px on any page.
- Every text and background pair in DESIGN.md meets 4.5:1 in both modes.

## Critical Paths
- A surfer opens `/` at 5:40am on a phone and gets the verdict, reason and hours without scrolling.
- An upstream failure never shows an old verdict as if it were current.
- The page carries a `Content-Security-Policy` of `default-src 'self'`, no inline script or style, and no `Set-Cookie`.

## Deploy checklist (cannot run in CI)
- In Cloudflare, Always Online is off and no cache rule serves stale HTML on an origin error. Stop the origin for five minutes and confirm the page becomes an error, not the last verdict.
- The health check allows up to 12 seconds after a start.
- A response header check on the live host shows `Cache-Control: public, max-age=0, s-maxage=120` on `/`, `/week` and `/about`, and no `Set-Cookie`.
- The verdict log volume is mounted and writable.
